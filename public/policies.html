Ordering and payment

Orders are subject to stock availability and verification. You receive a confirmation with your order number, items, sizes and total. Please enter a complete address and an active phone number. For high-value or cash-on-delivery orders RLVNT may confirm by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch. Seasonal and limited drops are sold while supplies last. Promo codes cannot be combined unless a campaign says so. Size changes, address corrections or cancellations are possible before the parcel is handed to the courier.

Shipping and delivery

Every order is packed in protective garment packaging. Tracking or a courier update is sent once the parcel ships. Please inspect the parcel on arrival and report visible damage to support with clear photos of the packaging and garment.

Returns, exchanges and refunds

Garments must be unworn, unwashed, free of perfume, stains or pet hair, in original RLVNT packaging with tags attached, and you need your order number. Size or style exchange: customer covers return shipping. Defective or wrong item: RLVNT covers shipping and offers a replacement or full refund after photo verification. Standard returns: refund after inspection, original and return shipping are non-refundable. Final-sale items, washed or altered garments and items missing tags are not returnable.

Sizing and care

RLVNT fits are relaxed and oversized. Check the size chart before checkout. Wash inside-out, cold (30°C max), gentle cycle, similar colors. Mild liquid detergent only: no bleach, stain removers or softeners. Hang dry or lay flat in shade. Iron inside-out on low heat, never directly on prints. Damage from improper washing, high heat, bleach or ironing over prints is not a manufacturing defect.

Privacy

RLVNT collects only your name, phone, email, delivery address and order history, used for orders, delivery, support and, if you opt in, drop announcements. We never sell or rent customer lists. Data is shared only with logistics and payment partners needed for your order. You can ask for access, correction or deletion at any time through support.
