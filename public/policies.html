Ordering and payment

Add pieces to your cart, press Place order, choose a size for each piece and enter your name, phone, city and address. Payment is cash on delivery only. Your order goes straight to RLVNT, and we may confirm by phone or WhatsApp before dispatch. Orders are subject to stock and verification. Seasonal and limited drops are sold while supplies last. All sizes are available. Promo codes cannot be combined unless a campaign says so.

Shipping and delivery

Flat shipping fee: 100 EGP per order. Delivery takes 3 to 5 days. Every order is packed in protective garment packaging. Please check the parcel on arrival and contact support quickly, with clear photos, if it is damaged.

Returns and exchanges

Send your request within 3 to 5 days after delivery. Garments must be unworn, unwashed, free of perfume, stains or pet hair, in original RLVNT packaging with all tags, and you need your order number. Size or style exchange: you cover return shipping. Defective or wrong item: RLVNT covers shipping and offers a replacement or refund after photo check. Standard returns: original and return shipping are not refunded. Final-sale items, washed or altered garments and items without tags cannot be returned.

Garment care

Wash inside-out, cold (30°C max), gentle cycle, similar colors. Mild liquid detergent only: no bleach, stain removers or softeners. Hang dry or lay flat in shade. Iron inside-out on low heat, never directly on prints. Damage from improper washing, high heat, bleach or ironing over prints is not a manufacturing defect.

Privacy

We collect only your name, phone, email (if you give one), delivery address and order details, used to process and deliver your order and for support. We never sell or rent customer lists. Data is shared only with delivery partners needed for your order. Ask for access, correction or deletion any time through support.
